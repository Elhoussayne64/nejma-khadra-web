Privacy Policy — Nejma Khadra

Developer: ElAr Games
Application: Nejma Khadra — نجمة خضرا (com.nejmakhadrastudio.nejmakhadra)
Last updated: November 24, 2026

ElAr Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Nejma Khadra collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. No account or personal profile

Nejma Khadra does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any login. Your progress, coins, cards, and settings are saved on your device.

2. Online features (Unity Gaming Services)

To back up your progress and show leaderboards, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, and Leaderboards), provided by Unity Technologies:

  • Anonymous sign-in: the game creates a random player ID. It is not linked to your name, email, or Google account.
  • Player name: a random nickname is generated automatically and shown on leaderboards. You can generate a different one in the game.
  • Cloud Save: a copy of your game progress is stored so it can be restored.
  • Leaderboards: your score and player nickname are submitted to the all-time and weekly rankings.

These features are optional: the game works offline. See Unity's privacy policy.

3. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

Nejma Khadra is free and shows ads, including optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads, through Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, these providers may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information, and ad interaction data.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalized advertising, and passes your answer to the ad providers.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) privacy policy.

4. Analytics

The released game does not send gameplay analytics off your device. If anonymous gameplay statistics are added in a future update, they will only use a random install ID, never personal information, and you will be able to switch them off in Settings.

5. Notifications

If you switch on daily reminders, the game schedules them on your device. No data is sent for reminders. Android 13 and later will ask for your permission first, and you can turn them off at any time.

6. Sharing

When you share a result or a challenge, the game creates an image or link and hands it to the app you choose. We do not receive the content you share or the people you share it with.

7. Children

Nejma Khadra is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. To delete your online data (player ID, cloud save, and leaderboard entries), email us at the address below with the subject "Delete my data", and include your player nickname if you can. We will process the request within 30 days.

9. Security

Data sent to Unity Gaming Services and ad providers is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data:
Email: support@elarmobo.com
